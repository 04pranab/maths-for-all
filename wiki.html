01 · WHY

The idea

Games are the medium. Mathematics is the learning. Human values are the background.

Maths for All is a free learning space for children with different ways of seeing, hearing, moving, reading, concentrating and learning. We do not want to make mathematics smaller. We want to make it easier to enter.

02 · FEEL

How we design

Act first.Let the child encounter the mathematics through an activity.
Mistakes are information.A wrong attempt opens another path instead of closing the door.
Don't manufacture pressure.Speed matters only when the mathematics genuinely calls for it.
Let values emerge.Patience, fairness, curiosity and perseverance live inside the mechanics.

04 · FOR EVERYONE

Accessibility

Large targets, clear structure, keyboard access, readable text, calm feedback, repetition and reduced-motion support are design requirements.

Large targetsKeyboard friendlyNo tiny essentialsReduced motionRepeat freely
05 · TRUST

Privacy & research

Playing is not permission to collect research data. Research participation is explicitly opt-in.

Collected only with consentResearch events inside the agreed research boundary.
Not part of research collectionPasswords, precise location, camera or microphone data, uploaded files, advertising identifiers and fingerprinting.
06 · RESPONSIBILITY

Responsible AI

AI can help with design, implementation, debugging, tests, documentation and review. It does not become the authority for correctness, accessibility, privacy or educational quality.

Human review and physical testing remain part of the build.
