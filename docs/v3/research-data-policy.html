Research Data Collection Policy

Status: This document defines the research-data collection contract for Maths for All. It applies to any deployment that enables research collection.

Compliance declaration: Research collection is authorised only when this policy is followed. A deployment that cannot satisfy this policy must keep research collection disabled. Applicable privacy and data-protection law may impose additional obligations.
Research collection is opt-in. No explicit research consent means no research event.

Consent rule

A user must be shown a clear research-data explanation and must actively choose whether to participate.

  • Yes: the application may collect only the research fields listed below.
  • No: the application must collect none of the listed research fields.
  • Login/account creation does not count as consent.
  • Silence, closing the dialog, or continuing to use the games does not count as consent.
  • The preference can be changed later from privacy/research settings.
  • Switching from Yes to No immediately stops future research collection and removes locally stored research events.

What we may collect after explicit Yes

CategoryExamples
Game/moduleArithmetic Quiz, Racing, Sudoku, Shape Fitting, Slab Maths
Event typeGame opened, answer submitted, hint requested, explanation requested, retry, completion
Learning stateDifficulty level, correct/incorrect result, attempt count
TimingTime spent on an answer or game event
Game progressQuestion, round, or level completion
Technical event timestampTime at which the research event occurred

The exact fields must remain tied to documented research questions. Future fields require a review of this policy and the consent wording.

What we do not collect as research data

  • Passwords or authentication secrets
  • Password hashes from the client
  • Email addresses unless a separate account/privacy requirement explicitly requires them
  • Phone numbers
  • Home or precise physical location
  • Contacts
  • Private messages or free-form personal notes
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browser fingerprinting data
  • Precise device identifiers
  • Camera, microphone, or files
  • Data from other applications
  • Research events when consent is No

Existing local progress

Existing local progress/analytics functionality must remain behind the same consent gate so the application does not silently continue recording research-style events after a user declines.

Data minimization

Prefer derived, non-identifying measurements over raw content. Where possible, record correctness, attempt count, response time, difficulty, and completion rather than unnecessary copies of question text or personal input.

Withdrawal

A user can change the preference at any time. When research collection is disabled, future research events are rejected, locally stored research events are deleted, research exports are disabled, and the educational application continues to work normally.

Compliance and accountability

The project author treats this policy as a binding project requirement for the official implementation: research collection must not be enabled in a way that contradicts the policy. Operators of modified or redistributed deployments are responsible for their own collection practices, notices, consent mechanisms, retention, security, and legal compliance. A failure to follow this policy may constitute a breach of the software license and may also create responsibility under applicable privacy or data-protection law. This statement does not create legal obligations beyond those imposed by applicable law.

Policy changes

New research-data categories or materially different research purposes require a policy update and corresponding consent-language review before they are enabled.

Transparency requirement

The consent UI must use plain language and provide two clearly available choices: Allow research data collection and Do not allow research data collection. It must include a concise collected/not-collected explanation and access to this full policy.

Implementation requirement

No game module may write research events directly to localStorage, a future database API, or a future telemetry API. All research writes must pass through the consent-aware research event gateway.
