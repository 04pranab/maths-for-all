Research Data Collection Policy

Research collection is opt-in. Login does not mean consent. Choosing No means no research data is collected.

What we may collect if you choose Yes

CategoryExamples
Game/moduleArithmetic Quiz, Math Racing, Sudoku, Shape Fitting, Slab Maths
Event typeGame opened, answer submitted, hint, explanation, retry, completion
Learning stateDifficulty, correct/incorrect result, attempt count
TimingTime spent on an answer or event
ProgressQuestion, round, or level completion
Technical timestampTime at which the research event occurred

What we do not collect as research data

  • Passwords or authentication secrets
  • Phone numbers or precise location
  • Contacts or private messages
  • Camera, microphone, or files
  • Advertising identifiers or browser fingerprinting
  • Research events when consent is No

Changing your choice

You can change the preference at any time from Privacy & research. If you switch to No, future research events are rejected and locally stored research events are removed. The educational games continue to work.
